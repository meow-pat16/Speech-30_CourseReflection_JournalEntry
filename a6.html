Even before the start of our speech class, I was quite nervous to take it because of the tight midyear schedule I had gotten (I had a class at iMath directly before) but also primarily because I feel like I haven’t been as confident in my own abilities and in conveying my own thoughts and ideas since I’ve started college. I was nervous to not be able to connect much with classmates because I would always be late (which was fortunately not the case), and I was nervous that I wouldn’t be able to articulate the things I wanted to say well enough…even in simply choosing topics for particular speeches I had a good amount of resistance and overthinking during the process. As days went on though and well into the class meetings, I fortunately realized that this wouldn’t exactly be the case and found myself growing more and more comfortable again with sharing in class, especially speech class. I really really appreciated the emphasis given in class, to which the focus is the ability to effectively communicate and convey thoughts and ideas, rather than only the technicalities like language, fluency, specific adherence to historic frameworks, etc. I believe it played a big role in letting my “guard” down (if that’s what you’d call it) in class.

As class sessions and other larger activities passed, I found myself enjoying more and more which made me quite glad. I had connected with more classmates through group activities such as the ProdAd and the Speech Writing Workshop, and enjoyed being able to exchange thoughts and feedback even on topics that aren’t particularly yours but you have something to add or share about. With all the new types of challenges conquered and encountered through the class, some unexpected and new while others familiar but still challenging, I’m really glad I was able to take and finish speech 30 this mid year term (dare I say successfully). I feel like after this class I’ve grown even just a little bit more toward being more confident with my own voice again, and not simply hiding myself in the shadows of my peers. On the other hand, while also understandable given the tighter midyear schedule, I feel like a lot of lighter, less grade/commitment heavy activities were missed though, short activities which help foster skills during in class lectures more—like our build-a-speech activity—and could be incorporated better during regular semester classes. All in all, all I can really say is I enjoyed a lot and learned a lot through Speech 30 and am really glad to have taken our class! Thank you for the term po Mx. Manna!
Audrey
